# Zero — Privacy Policy

**Zero doesn't collect anything.**

Your tasks, projects, and settings are stored in a single file on your Mac. Zero never sends them anywhere. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, and the app makes no network connections of any kind.

If you install Zero through the App Store or TestFlight, Apple may share standard, anonymised crash reports and installation statistics with the developer. That is governed by Apple's own privacy policy and the consent settings on your device, and it is the only information that ever reaches us.

Questions? Email [josh@evra.design](mailto:josh@evra.design)

*Effective 30 July 2026. If Zero ever changes how it handles data, this page will change first.*
